About Me

I am a PhD student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), specializing in computer vision and robotics.

My research focuses on intelligent navigation systems, 3D perception, and autonomous robotic systems. I develop algorithms that enable robots to understand and navigate complex environments through computer vision and deep learning.

↗ I am interested in practical, generalizable perception and navigation systems that connect visual understanding with embodied action.
