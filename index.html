About

I am a PhD student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), specializing in computer vision and robotics.

My research focuses on intelligent navigation systems, 3D perception, and autonomous robotic systems. I work on developing algorithms that enable robots to understand and navigate complex environments through advanced computer vision techniques and deep learning approaches.
