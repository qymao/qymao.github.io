About Me

I am currently a PhD student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), having graduated with a Bachelor's degree from Jilin University in 2019. My research focuses on computer vision and robotics, with a particular emphasis on developing intelligent navigation systems and efficient 3D perception algorithms.

My research work encompasses object navigation, multi-modal 3D object detection, and Transformer-based visual understanding in cutting-edge fields. By combining deep learning and robotics methodologies, I aim to advance the autonomous capabilities of intelligent robots in complex environments.

Research Interests

Robot Navigation

Deep learning-based intelligent navigation systems, object navigation algorithms, path planning and obstacle avoidance

Computer Vision

3D object detection, multi-modal perception, visual understanding and scene analysis

Deep Learning

Transformer architectures, attention mechanisms, end-to-end learning systems
